04 · What gets recorded

If I said it, it's in the brain.

  1. Every meeting · a recorder runs all day
  2. Every thought that hits · a voice note to one WhatsApp group
  3. Every coaching session
  4. Podcasts and interviews
  5. Big decisions · and why I made them
  6. Posts that worked and posts that flopped
  7. Personal sessions · only what you're comfortable storing

05 · The file

Six parts. One page. Claude reads it every time.

  1. Fixed facts · the numbers that can never be wrong
  2. Voice · how you really talk, and what you never say
  3. Story bank · every story, with the real number
  4. Red lines · what never goes into a post
  5. Beliefs · what you'd argue for on a stage
  6. Results · what worked, what flopped, why

Step 2 · Fill it in, in 20 minutes

Let Claude interview you.

I want to build my Founder Brain: one file that teaches you how I think, talk and decide, so everything you write for me sounds like me.

The template is in this project. Interview me to fill it in.

Rules:
- One question at a time. Wait for my answer.
- Go section by section, 3 to 5 questions each.
- If my answer is vague, ask for the real example and the number.
- Use my exact words. Never polish them.
- If I paste meeting transcripts or voice notes, mine them first and only ask about what's missing.

When we're done, give me the full file filled in, with [X] wherever I had no answer.
