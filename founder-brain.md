## 2. My voice

How I actually talk. Fill this from real recordings, not from how you wish you sounded.

- 3 phrases I really say: [ ]
- How I open a story: [ ]
- How I land a story: [ ]
- Words I never use: [ ]

Writing rules:
- Short sentences. Simple words. One idea per post.
- Hooks under 10 words, with a number, a name or a year in the first line.
- Speak to the reader, not about myself. Max 3 "I" in a short post.
- Test every draft: would I say this out loud on a podcast? If not, cut it.

Never write these. They are how readers spot AI:
- Em dashes or double dashes
- "It's not X, it's Y" in any wording
- Opening with "Honestly", "Frankly" or "To be honest"
- Parentheses
- delve, crucial, pivotal, tapestry, robust, seamless, realm
- "Here's the thing", "Hope this helps", "Most people..."
